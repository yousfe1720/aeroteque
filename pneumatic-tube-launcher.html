Overview

A highly portable system built around a 160mm smooth-bore barrel and a 12 L accumulator pressurised to 12 bar, delivering instant ejection velocity from any open ground.

Specifications
Barrel
160 mm smooth bore, 1.5 m
Accumulator tank
12 L, 12 bar
Launch type
Pneumatic
Elevation

Exit velocity
21 m/s
System weight
On request
Key features
01

160 mm launch tube

Smooth-bore barrel with a rear sealing flange and 3D-printed sabots for low-friction ejection.

02

12 L accumulator

Steel air tank charged with field PCP compressors for rapid pneumatic expansion.

03

Adjustable bipod

Folding legs give steady elevation from 30° to 45° launch trajectory.

04

Manual release

High-flow valve with an aluminium foil burst disc for instantaneous launch.
