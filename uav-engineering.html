Complete UAV engineering

One team, from first sketch to flying prototype

Integrated airframes, from mission analysis and aerodynamics through structures and propulsion to onboard installation.

Aerodynamics, structure, propulsion and installation are carried by the same engineers, on the same model.

What we deliver

Eight engineering services, available on their own or as one program

01Concept

Mission analysis & feasibility sizing

Payload, range, endurance and operating environment are turned into a realistic aircraft architecture. Preliminary sizing shows whether the target combination is achievable before large amounts of CAD work are created.

  • Requirements and constraint definition
  • Configuration trade study: fixed-wing, VTOL, flying wing, multirotor
  • Weight, power and energy budgeting
  • Preliminary sizing and performance estimate
02Aerodynamics

Aerodynamic design & performance analysis

Airfoil selection, planform and control-surface design, backed by CFD and flight-dynamics analysis, so the aircraft is efficient and controllable across its whole envelope.

  • Airfoil and planform optimisation
  • CFD and drag build-up
  • Centre of gravity and static margin
  • Static and dynamic stability, control authority
  • Range, endurance, climb and glide prediction
03Structures

Structural & composite engineering

Complete external and internal structure designed around loads, mass and manufacturing method, from carbon fibre monocoque to industrial 3D-printed and hybrid builds.

  • Load cases and finite-element analysis
  • CFRP layup and sandwich construction
  • Industrial FDM and hybrid structures
  • Mass and stiffness budgeting
  • Modular payload bays, hatches and mounts
04Propulsion

Propulsion & energy systems

Electric and EDF propulsion matched to the airframe and the mission, with the battery and power system sized to deliver the endurance that was promised.

  • Motor, propeller and ESC matching
  • EDF duct and inlet integration
  • Battery and power-system sizing
  • VTOL lift system and transition design
  • Efficiency and thermal budgeting of the propulsion chain
05Installation

Onboard systems installation & packaging

Avionics, batteries and payloads packaged into the airframe so they fit, balance and can be reached: the physical side of integration, designed together with the structure.

  • Equipment bays, trays and vibration isolation
  • Harness routing and connector access
  • Antenna and sensor placement on the airframe
  • Centre-of-gravity control with installed systems
  • Service access, hatches and maintainability
06Design

CAD, digital mock-up & design documentation

The whole aircraft modelled in 3D before anything is cut or laid up, so interfaces, masses and tolerances are settled on screen and carried into drawings.

  • Parametric 3D CAD of airframe and internals
  • Digital mock-up and interference checks
  • Mass properties and interface control
  • Drawings, bill of materials and revision control
07Build

Prototyping & build

From the first static demonstrator to a flying prototype: fast builds, fit checks and the design changes that follow, iterated in days instead of months.

  • Static demonstrator and rapid prototypes
  • Assembly, finishing and weighing
  • Fit checks and CG verification
  • Design iteration between builds
08Delivery

Production, export & lifecycle support

Manufacturing-ready files and documentation, export licensing and operational handover, with support continuing while the platform is in service.

  • Manufacturing-ready files and documentation
  • Export licensing, ICP and EUC compliance
  • Operational handover and training
  • In-service support and upgrades
