Prove it before it matters

Find the failure on the bench, not in the customer's hands

Simulation, test electronics and structured test engineering back every claim about performance and reliability with measured evidence.

Simulation removes risk early, hardware-in-the-loop tests the real avionics, and field tests confirm results against the brief.

What we deliver

Eight services that turn a design into evidence

01Simulation

Simulation-first development

The aircraft flies thousands of times before it flies once: flight dynamics, sensors, environment and failures modelled so design decisions are tested early and cheaply.

  • Six-degree-of-freedom flight dynamics models
  • Software-in-the-loop with PX4 and ArduPilot
  • Sensor, wind and failure modelling
  • Monte Carlo and envelope-edge scenarios
02Test electronics

Test electronics & instrumentation

The electronics that make verification possible: fixtures, interface boards and data-acquisition hardware built around the system under test, so every signal that matters can be measured and logged.

  • Test fixtures, breakout and interface boards
  • Signal conditioning for HIL interfaces
  • Data-acquisition and logging hardware
  • Design-for-test reviews: test points, debug and programming access
03Hardware-in-the-loop

Hardware-in-the-loop & bench validation

Real flight hardware connected to a simulated aircraft and world, so the exact avionics and software that will fly are tested on the bench.

  • HIL test rigs for flight controllers and companion computers
  • Instrumented bench with oscilloscopes and calibrated supplies
  • Signal-integrity and power-quality measurement
  • Automated regression tests
04Environment

EMI & environmental testing

Verification that the system keeps working when it is hot, cold, vibrating, wet or surrounded by radio noise.

  • EMI and radio-coexistence checks
  • Vibration and shock assessment
  • Temperature and humidity planning
  • Ingress and endurance testing
05Mechanical

Propulsion & structural testing

Measured, not assumed: thrust, power, strength and stiffness verified on dedicated rigs before they are trusted in the air.

  • Thrust-stand and efficiency testing
  • Static load and proof testing of structures
  • Battery discharge and thermal testing
  • Correlation with analysis models
06Flight test

Flight test campaigns

Structured campaigns from the first hover or glide to full-envelope operation, run to a written plan with instrumentation that answers the questions asked.

  • Test plans, safety cases and risk assessment
  • Onboard instrumentation and telemetry
  • Incremental envelope expansion
  • Field operations and support
07Data

Data analysis & performance verification

Flight logs turned into engineering decisions: what the aircraft actually did, compared with what the brief required.

  • Log processing and anomaly analysis
  • Performance versus requirements
  • Design-change requests driven by flight data
  • Test reports and recommendations
08Quality

Qualification, traceability & documentation

Test evidence that stands up to a customer, an auditor or a licensing authority, produced under an ISO 9001 certified quality system.

  • Requirements traceability and verification matrices
  • Configuration and change control
  • Acceptance test procedures
  • Records for export and regulatory submissions
Verification toolchain

From model to bench to range, one traceable chain of evidence

Each level of testing uses the same requirements and the same data formats, so results carry forward instead of being repeated.
