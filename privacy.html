Privacy Policy.

How Aeroteque Ltd collects, uses and protects your personal data.

Contents01Who we are02Information we collect03How we use your information04Legal bases05Sharing your information06International transfers07Retention08Your rights09Cookies10Security11Changes to this policy12Contact us

01Who we are

Aeroteque Ltd (company number 11357887) is registered in the United Kingdom and based in London. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

02Information we collect

We collect the details you give us when you contact us or apply for a role, such as your name, work email, organization and message. We also collect basic technical data about visits to this website, including device type, pages viewed and approximate location.

03How we use your information

We use your information to reply to inquiries, prepare quotes, assess job applications, keep our website secure and understand how it is used. We do not sell personal data.

04Legal bases

We process personal data where it is needed for a contract or to take steps before one, where we have a legitimate interest in running and improving our business, where we must meet a legal obligation, or where you have given consent.

05Sharing your information

We share personal data only with service providers who help us run the website and our systems, with professional advisers, and with authorities where the law requires. These providers act on our instructions.

06International transfers

If personal data is transferred outside the United Kingdom, we use safeguards recognized by UK data protection law, such as adequacy regulations or approved standard contractual clauses.

07Retention

We keep inquiries for as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period afterwards. Job applications are kept for up to twelve months unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

08Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict your personal data, to object to certain processing and to receive a portable copy. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

09Cookies

This website uses only the cookies and similar technologies needed to make it work and to measure use in aggregate. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

10Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including access controls and encryption in transit. No system is completely secure, so we review our measures regularly.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

12Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy can be sent to privacy@aeroteque.com or by post to Aeroteque Ltd, London, United Kingdom.
